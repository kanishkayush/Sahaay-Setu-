Where the roles land

Canvas--surface #f5f5f7 — the iOS grouped-background field every card lifts off.
Cards--bg #ffffff at --radius-lg 18px.
Dark chapter--fg #1d1d1f, one per screen, carrying the EMI figure or the primary action.
Accent--accent #0071e3, at most twice per screen: one filled action plus the active tab tint.
Status--success / --warn / --danger mixed into the surface, always beside a text label.

What actually changed.

Areav0 Expo appRebuilt
Palette Institutional blue #0B4F6C on hero cards, step markers and tabs, plus an orange #C25E00 accent surface. Neutral ladder carries hierarchy; a single blue accent, capped at two uses per screen.
Icons Emoji as functional icons — 🏠 📋 🧮 📍 👤 in the tab bar, 💬 🧮 📍 in the tiles. A 24-unit monoline SVG set, one stroke weight, sized 17 / 22 / 26.
Type Platform default sans at a 16px baseline. SF Pro Display / Text at 17px, with SF Mono reserved for figures.
Numbers Proportional digits — amounts shift width as a slider moves. Tabular mono throughout, so the EMI figure stays put while you drag.
Android One React Native layout rendered on both platforms. Separate files with Material chrome: app bar, nav pill indicator, FAB, outlined fields, full-screen dialogs.
Unchanged Copy, scheme figures, partner records, rule outcomes, the amortisation engine, and the prototype-data disclosures — all carried over as-is.

Known limitations

Scheme figures

Loan limits, rates and moratorium windows come from the v0 seed catalogue and are not yet verified against NSFDC circulars. Every screen keeps the notice.

Partner directory

Branch names, phone numbers, NPA percentages and fund figures are synthetic. Institution logos are deliberately absent — a real bank mark on a fabricated branch would mislead.

Map

The locator draws a schematic street view, labelled as such. No survey map tiles are bundled; distances are computed from the fixture coordinates against Pune 411001.
